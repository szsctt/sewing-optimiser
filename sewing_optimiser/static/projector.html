Projector

H hides this panel. Arrow keys move the layout 100 mm (Shift: 10 mm) to project it in sections. Click the projected area first if a box in this panel is selected.

Lay a cutting mat or tape under the projector. Enter the size of a rectangle you can see on it, then drag the four coloured handles until the projected rectangle's corners sit on its corners. The grid lines are apart.

Measure the projected square. If it is not , enter what you measure and the scale is corrected.
