Projector

H hides this panel. Arrow keys move the layout 100 mm (Shift: 10 mm) to project it in sections.

Lay a cutting mat or tape under the projector. Enter the size of a rectangle you can see on it, then drag the four green handles until the projected rectangle's corners sit on its corners. The grid lines are 100 mm apart.

Measure the projected square. If it is not 100 mm, enter what you measure and the scale is corrected.
