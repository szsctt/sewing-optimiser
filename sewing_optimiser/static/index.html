Pick pieces

Click each closed region that is a pattern piece for your size. Click again to deselect. Regions that touch are joined into one piece, with anything they enclose. Where nested sizes share an edge, click every strip from the smallest size out to yours. If the pattern also comes with size layers (for example an A4 file), choosing the size there is quicker.

Pieces

Untick option pieces you are not making. Pictures are turned so the grainline runs down the page.

Lines inside a piece, such as "cut at the shorter line for cuffs", split it into parts, shown dashed. To cut a shorter or narrower option, leave its extra part off.
